I'm Tomas Valenta, CTO and co-founder of E2B.
We met 18 years ago in middle school.
We learned programming together, created a bunch of video games,
and then went into DevTools,
trying to create things that we would actually use and help the whole community.
Over the time, we worked on more than 100 projects.
Each project was more and more serious.
Each got more and more users.
When we first saw LLAMS, we immediately knew that AI will be big
and that it will need sandboxes.
There was not a product market fit.
It was super early.
And we just knew that if AI were to get big,
the agents will need a platform to run.
We moved to San Francisco and worked on the platform for two years
without having a clear market fit
because we just knew the machines will be needed.
In the end, we were not too early, but just on time.
