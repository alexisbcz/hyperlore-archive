## Description

Flo Crivello raised about $50M as a solo founder, then watched the market kill the company he'd built. Instead of walking away, he cut deep, pivoted alone, and rebuilt into Lindy — one of the top AI agent startups. This is the full playbook: knowing when to kill a company and how deep to cut, why momentum beats rest when you're burned out, and how a solo founder builds a brain trust so the work never gets lonely.
